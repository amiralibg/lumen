Four directions for lumen.dev

Each page is fully interactive: the settings button (sliders icon, top right) changes accent, theme, look, density, motion speed and reduced motion, and can show light and dark side by side. Pick one and it becomes the whole site.

A · EtherealNight sky. A dithered aurora that follows your pointer, frosted glass, and light as the only material. Closest to CUI's mood, pushed further.Signature: live dither field · cursor glow on cards
B · AnthropomorphicThe components are characters. Lumi, the mascot, is built from switches and a slider, follows your cursor, blinks and reacts to what you click.Signature: Lumi · chunky outlines that squash on press
C · Conceptual SketchA designer's notebook on graph paper, with blueprint at night. Wireframes ink themselves into real components. A Specs toggle redlines the real numbers.Signature: sketch → ink · Specs mode
D · Scrapbooklumen's maker journal. Real renders as polaroids, washi tape, label-maker strips and sticky notes. You can pick up and move anything on the collage.Signature: draggable collage · Dymo labels
